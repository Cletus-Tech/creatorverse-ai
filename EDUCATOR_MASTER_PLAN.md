# CreatorVerse Academy — Educator Master Plan

**Status:** Draft curriculum; not deployed or published. All work is on the separate education-build branch.
**Teaching philosophy:** Project-based, guided and competency-led. Learners must be able to perform each skill independently, not merely watch a lesson.

## Delivery model
Every lesson follows: preparation → educator explanation → worked demonstration → guided practice → independent task → feedback → knowledge check. Each course includes a final portfolio artefact.

### Required anatomy of every chapter
- Measurable learning outcomes, prerequisites, duration and class format.
- Full teaching notes with concepts, steps, examples and decision rules.
- Key vocabulary explained plainly.
- Educator demonstration script and worked example.
- Guided practice, independent assignment and expected submission.
- Common mistakes, troubleshooting and quality checklist.
- Knowledge check with answer key.
- Assessment rubric and curated resources.
- Accessibility, safety, copyright, privacy and ethical considerations.

### Weekly delivery
Standard 5-hour week: 60 min teaching, 60 min demonstration, 90 min guided lab, 60 min independent project, 30 min review/assessment. YouTube Automation uses 6 hours weekly, adding a 60-minute production clinic. Provide phone-friendly, low-data and offline alternatives.

### Assessment
Formative checks in every lesson; a practical stage assignment; a mid-course review; and a final capstone. Suggested pass: 70%, with all critical safety, factual accuracy and copyright safeguards met. Feedback must identify strengths, corrections and the next action. No course promises income, virality, platform approval or employment.

## Course delivery plans and curriculum maps

### 1. YouTube Automation: Build a Faceless Channel
Beginner–Intermediate | 4 months | 6 hours/week.
- Stage 1 — Strategy, niche and channel foundations: YouTube business model and automation workflow; choose a niche using evidence; define audience, positioning and content pillars.
- Stage 2 — Research, scripting and production: research topics and build an idea backlog; fact-checking and source documentation; write scripts that retain attention.
- Stage 3 — Packaging, publishing and growth: voiceover, visuals and copyright-safe assets; video editing and production quality control; titles, thumbnails and honest packaging.
- Month 4 capstone: production calendar, repeatable upload checklist, metadata, moderation, analytics interpretation, iteration and a complete pilot package.

### 2. AI Content Creation: From Idea to Finished Media
Beginner | 3 months | 5 hours/week.
- Stage 1 — Content strategy and AI foundations: AI content workflows and tool selection; audience research and content pillars; prompt briefs and brand voice systems.
- Stage 2 — Writing, visuals and audio production: research and fact-checked writing; AI image direction and visual consistency; voice, audio and narration production.
- Stage 3 — Quality, distribution and measurement: AI video storyboarding and editing; repurpose content across platforms; copyright, privacy and responsible AI.
Capstone: a researched campaign with written, visual, audio and video assets plus a distribution plan.

### 3. TikTok Growth & Creator Monetisation
Beginner–Intermediate | 3 months | 5 hours/week.
- Stage 1 — Creator positioning and content system: creator identity and profile positioning; audience research and repeatable content series; hooks and short-form storytelling.
- Stage 2 — Short-form production and discovery: mobile filming, framing and lighting; editing, captions and sound design; search, trends and discoverability.
- Stage 3 — Analytics, partnerships and revenue: community building and moderation; creator analytics and controlled experiments; media kits, pricing and brand outreach.
Capstone: positioned creator profile, 30-day content system, analytics review and brand partnership kit. Monetisation eligibility varies by country, age, account standing and current platform rules.

### 4. Prompt Engineering: Communicate with AI Like a Pro
Beginner–Advanced | 2 months | 5 hours/week.
- Stage 1 — Prompt foundations and clear instructions: how generative AI models interpret prompts; anatomy of a professional prompt; context, examples and structured outputs.
- Stage 2 — Advanced and multimodal workflows: break complex tasks into stages; research prompts and evidence requirements; multimodal image and video prompting.
- Stage 3 — Evaluation, safety and reusable systems: debug prompts and evaluate outputs; privacy, safety and hallucination control; build reusable prompt templates.
Capstone: tested prompt library, evaluation log and repeatable workflow.

### 5. AI Graphic Design & Visual Branding
Beginner–Intermediate | 3 months | 5 hours/week.
- Stage 1 — Design fundamentals and brand systems: visual hierarchy, composition and layout; colour theory and accessible contrast; typography and information design.
- Stage 2 — AI-assisted visual production: brand strategy and identity systems; logo concepts and vector refinement; flyers, posters and campaign layouts.
- Stage 3 — Professional exports and portfolio: AI image generation and photo editing; social media design systems; print and digital export standards.
Capstone: brand identity, campaign designs and production-ready export pack.

### 6. Digital Business: Build, Market & Sell Online
Beginner–Intermediate | 3 months | 5 hours/week.
- Stage 1 — Find and validate a business opportunity: customer problems and market discovery; business models and offer design; competitor research and differentiation.
- Stage 2 — Build the offer and acquire customers: validate demand and find first customers; pricing, costs and unit economics; build trust with a landing page.
- Stage 3 — Sales, operations and sustainable growth: content marketing and lead generation; sales conversations and proposals; customer onboarding and service delivery.
Capstone: validated offer, pricing model, landing page, sales proposal and service-delivery process.

### 7. Algorithm Analysis & Profitable Niche Research
Intermediate | 2 months | 5 hours/week.
- Stage 1 — Recommendation systems and platform signals: how recommendation systems work; audience satisfaction and content fit; search intent and algorithm myths.
- Stage 2 — Niche, demand and competitor research: analytics definitions and measurement; demand research and topic validation; competitor mapping and content gaps.
- Stage 3 — Testing, analytics and decision-making: niche scoring and opportunity selection; controlled experiments and analytics review; write an evidence-led niche opportunity report.
Capstone: evidence-backed niche report, scoring model and controlled experiment plan.

### 8. 3D Animation
Beginner | 2 months target | 5 hours/week.
The currently published record has one module and one lesson labelled “Content monetization”; this is not a valid 3D curriculum. Preserve the course record and learner progress, but replace the curriculum only after a draft is reviewed.
- Stage 1 — Blender foundations and scene building: interface, navigation and project setup; mesh modelling and edit-mode fundamentals; transforms, modifiers, object organisation and scene composition.
- Stage 2 — Materials, lighting and animation: materials and UV basics; camera, lighting and rendering; keyframes, timeline, graph editor and animation principles.
- Stage 3 — Character/action and final production: rigging fundamentals and safe use of existing rigs; walk/action blocking and timing; render, sound, editing and final short-scene delivery.
Capstone: original short 3D scene. Teach viewport optimisation and render settings for modest hardware.

## Learning assets required for every course
Course overview; learner profile; prerequisites; outcomes; glossary; stage/chapter schedule; complete lesson notes; educator board/slide outline; demonstration; guided and independent practice; templates; checklists; low-bandwidth alternatives; knowledge checks and answer keys; stage assignments; capstone brief; marking rubric; curated references with publisher, URL, purpose and review date.

## Resource and quality policy
Prefer official documentation, platform creator education, standards bodies, reputable textbooks and primary research. Third-party tutorials are supplementary. Never fabricate a source or tool capability. Mark volatile platform rules and software interfaces for review. Verify external links before publication.
