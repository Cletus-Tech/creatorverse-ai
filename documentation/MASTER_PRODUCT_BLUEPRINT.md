# CreatorVerse — Master Product Blueprint
Version 1.0 | Planning baseline | 2026-11-11

## Product standard
CreatorVerse is one professionally operated learning and student-life platform delivered through a responsive website and native-feeling Android/iOS apps. The first impression must communicate competence, clarity, trust and thoughtful engineering. No clutter, needless navigation layers, decorative noise, fake content, dead controls or misleading feature claims.

## Principles
1. One product, two first-class clients.
2. Shared identity, backend, data, permissions and business rules.
3. Clarity before decoration; every element earns its place.
4. Mobile-first performance for variable networks and modest devices.
5. Accessibility, privacy, security and moderation are foundational.
6. Admin-configurable content where safe; core security/business rules stay in code.
7. No fake listings, profiles, ratings, course outcomes or verification badges.
8. Preserve existing records; migrations require a documented backup and recovery plan.

## Three divisions
### Digital Skills
One entry into an extensible skills catalogue: categories, programmes, courses, learning paths, modules, lessons, resources, projects, assessments and pricing. AI learning remains a category, not the public product name.

### Academic
Secondary School: WAEC, NECO, JAMB, GCSE and approved additional examinations, with subjects, syllabus mapping, lessons, practice and mock exams as content is developed.
Tertiary Institutions: institution → faculty/school → department/programme → level → semester → course. Detailed academic model requires approval before implementation.

### Campus Life
Campus Accommodation: Find a Roommate, Find an Apartment, Become an Agent, Accommodation Community.
Further services: student marketplace, Past Questions & Study Hub, campus errands/delivery.
Emergency SOS is a separate project and excluded.

## Shared capabilities
Accounts/profiles, roles, discovery/search, enrolment and learning progress, community, messaging, notifications, payments, moderation, support, analytics, certificates, admin CMS, audit logs and privacy controls.

## Roles
Visitor, learner/student, creator/instructor/teacher, housing agent/partner, moderator/support, administrator and super administrator. Roles cannot be self-promoted. Sensitive actions require server-side checks and audit records.

## Website
Full public discovery and working platform, authenticated workspace, learning environments, community, campus services, support and central web-based Super Admin Control Board.

## Mobile
Flutter app for Android and iOS, designed as a real mobile product, not a webview. Android is the first release target while iOS compatibility is maintained from the beginning.

## Brand and opening experience
Redesigned original CreatorVerse identity: distinctive symbol and wordmark, scalable vector master, dimensional marketing render as an optional variant, app icon, favicon and light/dark/monochrome versions. Opening/loading uses a restrained branded motion mark or progress indicator—not emoji, not a long animation, never an indefinite blocker.

## Quality gates
A feature is not live until implemented and verified. Releases require functional, responsive/device, accessibility, security and performance checks plus updated documentation.

## Decisions requiring approval
Final logo/palette; landing copy and hero artwork; visitor preview permissions; tertiary data model/content licensing; payment providers/currencies/refunds; housing verification and inspection standards; community/age/moderation policies; retention/export/deletion; offline scope and push provider; admin permission tiers.
